A Pirate's Guide to the Badlands Cluster

OK, I'm sure that you're all already experts on everything, but knowledge is power, even for a pirate. I need to be sure that all my crewmen know what's what and who's who in the Badlands.

To this end, Lefty and I have compiled this handy encyclopedia. It's in five rough sections, and is fully cross-linked. It's up to you to read and digest this material, and become a better, wiser fighter.

You've now got all the info you need, so don't come crying to me if you find out the hard way that a Dreadnaught Corvette has rear firing guns, or that you can't sneak into a ship yard with your active sensors on. And if you pick on the wrong station and get chased all the way home by corporate security, it won't just be their guns waiting to greet you.

Hopefully, you'll learn and live,

-Lucrecia Johnston
